The story

Dance is a discipline of millimeters.

I grew up watching my sister train in Kuchipudi. Every posture, every mudra matters — and solo practice, the hours between classes, is where millimeters are quietly lost. Svarupa is the teacher’s eye for those hours.

01

The problem: practice alone, feedback never

Millions learn movement through screens — a guru on video call, a YouTube class, an Instagram lesson. Then they practice alone for a week with no idea whether their araimandi is deep enough or their arm is truly straight.

Teachers can’t watch every student’s home practice. So small errors calcify into habits, and habits into ceilings.

02

Live tracking, right in the browser

Point your camera and Svarupa reads 33 body landmarks in real time — skeleton overlay, eight joint angles, all computed on your device. No app install, no upload, no waiting.

What you see in the studio below is the actual working prototype, not a mockup.

03

The teacher sets the destination

Svarupa never replaces the guru — it amplifies her. In Teacher Record mode, a teacher holds any pose and captures it; those exact joint angles become the reference students practice against.

The standard of “correct” always belongs to a human teacher, never to an algorithm.

04

One correction at a time

Eight joints are scored every frame, but you only ever hear about the single biggest deviation — “sink lower through both knees.” Hold 85%+ for two seconds and the pose is complete.

Like a good teacher: one clear instruction, never a barrage.

05

Progress you can feel accumulating

Every session tracks practice time, attempts, best match, and poses held. Students watch their percentage climb; teachers see the practice they could never see before.

Motivation, measured in millimeters closed.

06

Beyond one dance, beyond dance

Kuchipudi is the proving ground — the hardest test of precision. But the engine is discipline-agnostic: Bharatanatyam, ballet, yoga, physiotherapy, strength training.

Anywhere a body must learn a shape, a teacher’s eye can now be there for the lonely hours.
